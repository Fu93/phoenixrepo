## What this cut does not claim

- It does not claim that all 16 evaluation repositories went through Zetaris, or that `zetaris_check.py` wrote a verdict.
- It does not claim a CalendarSync verdict until the recorded run is on screen.
- It does not claim the revive loop fixed any repository.
- It does not read a number off a SAMPLE replay and call it the scored run.
- It does not say the scored run produced no reasoning, and it does not say the model did not reason. The 0 of 16 figure counts a separate reasoning field or a complete `<think>…</think>` pair. The scored traces recorded no reasoning text for the final verdict. The counter that wrote them recognised only a separate reasoning field or a complete `<think>…</think>` pair, and Nightshift's answers carry reasoning inline before a bare `</think>`, so the scored traces cannot say whether the model reasoned. The scored-run .env is not in the repo and the traces do not record request settings. The operator's configured fast-tier setting was enable_thinking=false; whether Nightshift applied it is unknown. Temperature 0 is the code default in app/agents/relevance.py and neither tier's extra body sets it. It does not say thinking was on or off for the scored run. Limit: no committed trace shows two live runs.
- The card heading on the replay page is "Reasoning field on this trace line". Show it only from a RECORDED replay whose title is `Experiment E3 (NVIDIA direct; final verdict only: thinking on, temperature 1.0; bulk steps thinking off, temperature 0), not the scored run`. Those two replays are imported: `e3-goodreasonai-scrapeserv` and `e3-dsregoitia-calendarsyncproject`. Do not show that card from a scored-run replay, and do not read an E3 verdict as the scored run. The committed rescue-plan trace also has a reasoning excerpt; do not show that card as the scored run or as E3.
- It does not call the Invoice replay a refusal. That recorded verdict is CANDIDATE, a disagreement. #46 is merged.
